# Offline throughput and safety change

This branch changes the scale coordinator only. No provider call, credential access, live queue, or budget ledger was used for these checks.

- Evidence requests are planned before the first paid reservation. Each planned request retains its exact rows, serialized body, size-specific configuration hash, and the existing full output-token reservation. The largest prefix of up to 25 reviews that passes the payload builder's conservative context bound is selected. Source order is stable. A single review that cannot fit is saved with `evidence_context_limit` on it and its exact-text aliases; no text is truncated and no request is reserved for it.
- A transient response sets a stage-local dispatch deadline. Existing futures continue to settle during that cooldown. New dispatch resumes after the deadline, subject to the existing eight-request global cap, Jev overload ceiling, uncertain-request holds, and quality halts. Paid POSTs are never retried automatically.
- On this host, planning 10,000 synthetic reviews of about 180 characters into 400 full 25-review requests took a median **0.068 s** across three runs. Building the 400 payloads directly took **0.050 s** median. The extra **0.018 s** measures offline preparation only; it does not predict live wall-time savings. A simulated 80 ms transient cooldown verified that other in-flight successes settled before a ninth dispatch, which waited at least 70 ms after the failed completion.
- Regression tests cover conservative bound acceptance and failure, long-text exclusion with source IDs intact, exact-text alias blocking, 10-review and 25-review tail identities, full reservations, in-flight settlement, worker throttle, and resume safety. The full synthetic suite passed with 258 tests. These tests do not establish model quality or live throughput.
